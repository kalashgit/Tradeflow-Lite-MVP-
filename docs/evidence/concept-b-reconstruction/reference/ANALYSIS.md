# Original nine-frame study

Source: founder upload `7FE35941-EE0E-489D-BC1E-6CCDB77B5E93.gif`, retained as source.gif. SHA256 is in the shipped manifest. 420 × 408; nine frames, 170 ms each; loop=0 (continuous), duration 1.53 s. Each decoded frame and a contact sheet are retained. Rebuild the textures and bidirectional flow using `python3 scripts/build-concept-b-reference.py` (offline development dependencies: Pillow, NumPy, OpenCV; none ship as browser dependencies).

- **Silhouette:** blue>60 bounds across the sequence span x=34…386, y=21…372. Frame 0 bounds are [41,29,377,363]; frame 8 is [36,23,380,364]. Local bulges migrate around the membrane. A circular clipping sphere would remove the target's uneven lobes.
- **Weave/curvature:** very fine crossed strands turn together across folds, gathering in compressed cyan ridges and opening into widely spaced recessed navy fibers. `texture-lighting-analysis.json` records a reproducible horizontal texture scan and illuminated-region statistics; it is a projected image measurement, not a claim of inferred physical mesh topology.
- **Fold progression:** top crest is broad in frame 0/1, contracts toward the upper left/center in frames 2/3, and produces a curved top pocket in frames 4/5. Frames 6/7 carry the strongest rising left fold and lower crest; frame 8 brings the bright crest across the lower-left edge toward the initial configuration. Rear and front fibers overlap; an independently animated ring cannot reproduce this.
- **Illumination/depth:** the crests carry cyan and sky blue; broad recessed regions remain navy with almost black central negative space. Brightness is distributed locally along the fabric rather than as a constant rim. Source fold lighting is retained as baked material; it is not reconstructed 3D light transport.
- **T:** thresholded central bright bounds are approximately x=163…273 and y=155…241 (roughly 110 × 84). The selected curved cap and tapering diagonal stem are retained directly from the source; no approximate SVG is overlaid. The center is locked out of the extra speech displacement.
- **Continuity:** measured optical flow for all consecutive frame pairs is in `public/orb-concept-b/manifest.json`. The 95th-percentile displacement varies around 27–58 pixels. These are image correspondences, not ground-truth material velocities.
- **Loop:** frame 8 → 0 has the greatest 95th-percentile flow (~58 px) and source pixel difference (~13.98/255 MAE). It receives its own forward/backward flow pair. Rendered samples 1 ms before/after wrap are compared separately; the output is time-based and interpolated instead of nine hard frame cuts.

The renderer transports this reference-derived material using a shared 48 × 48 deformation grid where vertex texture fetch is supported, otherwise a fragment transport path. Field smoothing reduces correspondence tearing. Disocclusions still require blending and can soften fibers at intermediate phases. A full 3D model with independently simulated folds and geometric lighting has **not** been recovered from these nine projections.
