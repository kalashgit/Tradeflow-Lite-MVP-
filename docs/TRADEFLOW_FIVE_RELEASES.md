# TradeFlow — Five Release Roadmap and Public Launch Gates

Design: Preserve TradeFlow's unique phone-first, dark high-contrast interface, navigation Home / Map / Opportunities / Finance / More and useful contextual AI. Do not copy Jobber's UI or add ornamental animation.

## Release 01 — Growth Intelligence (currently on preview)
- Editable Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) and buyer persona editor, separate profiles with links between them
- Growth and acquisition directory with explicit Available/Preview/Planned statuses
- Rule-based Business Missions create real local Tasks; no unattended AI execution
- Opportunity dossier includes sourced business contacts and native phone/email/message actions, with editable ICP assignment
- Not yet: automatic paid research/enrichment, account-scoped research credits, marketing automations

## Release 02 — Calendar and Field Operations (local foundations on preview)
- Day/week/month calendar of existing Jobs and Tasks; local scheduling and appointment records
- Field assessment/checklist forms and print, connected with real jobs where supported
- Not yet: shared calendar, route optimization, employee GPS location, cloud file storage, dispatch or signed customer portal

## Release 03 — Invoices and Job-Cost Review (local foundations on preview)
- Invoice drafts linked to customers, jobs and quotes, itemized amounts and custom tax-rate input
- Explicit local "issued" state only; no automatic send, verified card payment or certified tax-invoice promise
- Manually recorded received payments linked to invoices and ordinary Finance records
- Planned/actual manual direct job costs and linked Finance expenses; cash contribution marked incomplete, not final profit
- Current Finance denomination is EUR. Do not sell this as US-ready invoicing until per-customer currency, sales tax, provider, registration and statutory invoice requirements are addressed

## Release 04 — Marketing Studio (local foundation on preview)
- Implemented: editable campaign drafts linked to ICP/personas, local SEO and review request templates, manually copyable draft content, and campaign preparation Tasks
- Implemented: offline research brief editor with saved ICP/persona snapshot, strict evidence classifications, human QC requirements, downloadable JSON instructions, an exact-schema blank CSV import template, and linked review Tasks. No actual searches or enrichment runs.
- Planned: sophisticated SEO/ads management, analytics, customer-approved sending and account-scoped audiences
- Future paid lead research/enrichment/QA based on ICP and personas. Runs need identity, server quotas, source evidence, rejection logs, usage metering, deduplication and safe outreach
- No automated sending, paid ads, crawling, personalized targeting or charging yet

## Release 05 — Route Planner and Integration Readiness (local foundation on preview)
- Implemented: select saved geolocated Jobs, Tasks, Opportunities or Pins, store date-specific stop lists, suggest a nearest-neighbor order by straight-line distance, inspect locations on the existing map and open external Google Maps directions
- Implemented: an integration-status directory that accurately lists providers as not connected and does not collect credentials
- Not yet: road-distance optimization, travel times, crew GPS/location sharing, multiuser permissions, or real third-party synchronization
- Account identity / tenant isolation, permissions, encrypted storage and audit logs are prerequisites before team access
- Idempotent integration APIs, error queues and conflict handling for QuickBooks, payment systems, messaging, ads and routes
- Private customer hub, optional GPS/location-sharing with consent, crew planning and route scheduling after vendor/provider contracts

## Explicit launch gates independent of feature count
1. Define legal customer entity and refund/fulfilment terms; confirm the $199 one-time founding Core offer and precise 25-record evidence-backed research deliverable
2. Real cross-device account authentication, server-side authorization and tested data access separation before selling collaborative features
3. Verify jurisdiction-specific tax invoices, currency handling and bank/payment setup before claiming automatic invoices or receiving real payments through the app
4. Pass device-based end-to-end tests for registration, license, migration/backup, mobile map, customer records, quote→job, invoice and paid fulfilment
5. Privacy/compliance review of contact data, marketing consent, retention, review requests and sensitive job attachments
6. Document working vs preview vs roadmap features accurately; do not claim market readiness or zero recurring infrastructure costs simply because five modules are visible

Current user/market pilot: small Austin-area electrical contractors. Opportunity records must distinguish verified demand, potential opportunity, project signal and business prospect. All researched contacts must be appropriately sourced; do not invent intent, phone numbers or addresses.
