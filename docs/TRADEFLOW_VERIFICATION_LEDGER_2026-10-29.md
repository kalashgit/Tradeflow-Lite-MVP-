## Rules
- VERIFIED means a specific tool or executable check returned direct supporting evidence.
- SOURCE-COMMITTED means files were read back from GitHub; it does not establish passing CI or deployment.
- UNVERIFIED means no direct result; do not infer success.
- BLOCKED means a named dependency/access problem prevents verification.
- FAILED means an actual reproducible negative result.
- Never retire one of the 62 contractor pain points without end-to-end test evidence and an observed contractor scenario.
