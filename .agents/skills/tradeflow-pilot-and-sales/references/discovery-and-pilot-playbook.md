# Commercial Pilot and Sales Practice
## Three roleplay archetypes (illustrative)
A. Owner of 8-person commercial TI contractor: Excel+QuickBooks, labor numbers late; skeptical about double-entry.
B. Owner/GM of 14-person project+service firm: existing FSM already does estimates/scheduling; asks why another subscription.
C. Office coordinator/estimator: worried field staff won't enter time/materials and reports will be wrong.
Each simulation begins with one credible observed trigger but fictional identity and values. Roleplay the buyer, ask hard questions and do not give easy objections. Afterward score discovery (0–4), specificity (0–4), trust/evidence (0–4), competitive handling (0–4), next step (0–4).
## Owner interview opening
'We're studying how small commercial electrical contractors track labor, material spend and jobs ready to invoice. Could I understand how you do this today? I'm not assuming software is the answer.'
## Core objections
'We already have ServiceTitan/Jobber' → ask about *actual* job-cost visibility and burdens, do not claim incumbent lacks the feature.
'My technicians won't type anything' → show actual minimal input and measure adoption; do not promise zero effort.
'Will it work without signal?' → distinguish existing offline business records from unverified remote speech inference.
'Can the AI send invoices?' → only via existing reviewed commands and permissions once implemented and validated.
'I don't want to migrate' → test overlay/manual import and narrowly scoped pilot; quantify switching cost.
## Bounded paid offer
Define scope (one to three jobs), dates, users, fees, tax/refund/cancellation, customer data authorization, secure import methods, offline limitations, role separation, support obligations, exits/deletion and clear success criterion. Never assume buyer agrees to a price just because it appears in workbook.
## Roleplay debrief
Log exact objection, response, missed diagnosis, next question, hypothesis to verify, not 'lead won'.
## No unapproved actions
No actual outreach, invoices, subscription changes or promise made on behalf of TradeFlow without explicit authorized owner action.
