# Source-backed Customer Discovery
## Evidence levels
L0 fictional demo; L1 inferred industry context/secondary source; L2 directly stated by qualified buyer, with date and permission; L3 observed redacted workflow artifact; L4 observed paid commitment/ongoing use; L5 independently measured business outcome. Never promote levels silently.
## Austin qualification columns
Source/date | legal company/name | TDLR contractor status | active jurisdiction | verified employee band | commercial service/project mix | owner/GM | software stack | specific workflow pain | existing workaround | interview consent | disposition. Handle missing as UNKNOWN.
## Owner interview (no leading)
1. Tell me about the last small commercial job you completed.
2. How were hours recorded, attributed and checked against estimate?
3. How do supply/material receipts reach each job's cost view?
4. How are changes approved and billed?
5. What happens between completion and sending an invoice?
6. What is your present software/spreadsheet process?
7. When did it fail last, and how do you know? What did it cost?
8. Why haven't you changed the process?
9. Who would use/approve a new tool? What is acceptable field effort?
10. Which real result would make a paid pilot worthwhile?
## Market math
TAM=employer firms in specified NAICS and geographic scope, not CBP establishments mistakenly labeled firms. SAM=qualified firms by work mix/size/budget/workflow/software and deployment feasibility; avoid multiplication of correlated assumptions as though independent. Reachable pool=locally verified decision-makers with legal contact and capacity. Nonemployers and license counts separate.
## Experiments
20 interviews, 8 observed workflows, 8 qualified pilot offers are proposed cohorts. Gates 8/20 pain, 5/8 no-extra-admin improvement and 3/8 commitment are provisional experiment rules, not externally validated baselines.
## Deliverable
Compact confidence-scored buyer evidence table plus raw source links, disconfirming statements and explicit data still needed.
