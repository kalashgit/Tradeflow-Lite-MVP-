# TradeFlow Customer Discovery

Read `references/evidence-and-interviews.md`. Separate what **owners actually said and did** from secondary sources, our hypotheses and fictional examples.

## Market method
- Distinguish SUSB **firms**, CBP **establishments**, NES **nonemployers** and state **licenses**. Never add them without clear overlap rules. Census 5–9 plus 10–19 is 5–19 proxy, not exact 5–15.
- Keep **software buyers** (electrical contractors) distinct from **E01 project/permit leads** (potential work opportunities for those contractors).
- Austin company seeds need verification: legal identity, current operation, license, employee band, small-commercial work mix, owner contact, existing stack and buyer interest.
- The Nov 1 market report's TAM, local SAM, interview evidence, WTP and acquisition metrics remain unverified; synthetic funnel rates are not forecasts.

## Customer interview and observation
Use open non-leading questions about most recent projects: original estimate, labor entry, material receipts, approved extras, closeout and invoice. With consent, observe actual records; scrub PII. Ask for last time and frequency, lost time/money or uncertainty, workaround, competing tool, decision process and budget. Do not solicit sensitive contractor customer data unnecessarily.

## Decision gates
The prior research proposed **experimental**, not benchmark, gates: 8/20 target owners substantiate pain; 5/8 workflows measurably improve without new admin; 3/8 qualified pilots pay/commit. Refine by evidence, not by motivated reasoning.

## Output
Verified account register with sources and confidence, interview note format, disqualifications, pain frequency/severity table, incumbent comparison, quantified customer outcome if measured and explicit recommendation to revise/continue/stop.
