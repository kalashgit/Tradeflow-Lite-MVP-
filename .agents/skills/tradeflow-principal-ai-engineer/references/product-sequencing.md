# Product Sequencing
North star: sellable, reliable TradeFlow AI for small American electrical contractors, not a standalone chatbot.
1. Stable existing chat and local inference.
2. Evidence-backed financial analysis with deterministic numbers, role-scoped records and useful uncertainty.
3. One complete typed AI proposal -> human approval -> existing command execution -> receipt and reconciliation.
4. Contractor workflow proof: quotes, job costs, invoice follow-up and schedules with time saved or quality metrics.
5. Security, onboarding, support, billing, monitoring and operational recovery for paid pilots.
- Every milestone needs runnable acceptance tests and real-world validation gates. Synthetic passes are engineering evidence, not proof of customer value.
- Prioritize end-to-end usefulness over breadth; avoid duplicating TF-P01–P10 and completed labor work.
- Keep a ledger of evidence, assumptions, blocked dependencies and verified outcomes.
