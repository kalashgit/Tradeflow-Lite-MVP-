# Product Vision and Sequencing Contract
## Product thesis
One offline-first, AI-native business headquarters for smaller American electrical contractors; existing functional GUI plus Master Intelligence conversation by text and voice. Natural conversation is a layer, not an independent app. Business commands share approved audited infrastructure.
## Focused initial buyer
Owner/president/GM of a 5–15-person commercial TI/small-project and maintenance electrical firm near Austin; one company may operate in both segments. Wider 5–20 is screening band, residential tertiary. No validated TAM or pricing yet.
## Buyer job-to-be-done
"When commercial work is moving fast, show me which jobs are under control and what must be done before I can bill and collect—without sending my crew back to fill out another system."
## Minimum workflow
Select job → capture/associate labor and materials with provenance → compare to bid/budget and highlight missing inputs → review change-order state → assess completion/signoff → prepare invoice-ready checklist → owner review and approved command. Existing invoicing/permissions remain the source; never auto-issue or mark collected.
## Guardrails
Do not invent project margin when incomplete; no new competing account/workflow record store; no speculative self-initiating agents; preserve field offline operation and safe re-sync. Voice can be unavailable while offline; local business records must remain usable.
## Product decision record
Proposed feature → target task → verified cost/pain → incumbent comparator → simpler alternatives → 1 workflow test → negative/auth test → product gate → customer outcome gate → exclusion list.
## Pilot rule
Prefer measurable one-job improvements over entire-platform replacement; keep capability flags factual. Real contractor feedback is required to change defaults.
