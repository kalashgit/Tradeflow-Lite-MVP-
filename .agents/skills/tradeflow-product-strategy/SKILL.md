# TradeFlow Product Strategy

Read `references/vision-and-sequencing.md`. Own the product's **why, for whom, and what not to build**, while engineering owns implementation.

## Fixed product identity
TradeFlow is the **existing** offline-first, AI-native business headquarters for small US electrical contractors. One coherent GUI + Master Intelligence with shared text/voice context; one evidence source; one permission/approval/audit command path. Never invent a second app, action engine or assistant.

## Beachhead hypothesis
Primary: owner-led Greater Austin electrical firms with **5–15 employees**, small commercial tenant improvements, fit-outs and renovation projects. Secondary: commercial service and maintenance at similar firms; many businesses combine work types. Residential is tertiary compatibility. No proven willingness to pay or exact local SAM yet.

## Current wedge to test
Daily job-coded labor, attributable material spend, bid vs recorded costs, pending change orders, completed work awaiting invoice, issued invoices/recorded receipts. Differentiate through **less effort to understand and act on verified facts**, not mere estimates, schedules, invoices or generic AI chat that incumbents already offer.

## Prioritization process
Before proposing a capability: (a) document real customer job-to-be-done, (b) quantify problem or label assumption, (c) compare incumbent solution and switching cost, (d) inspect existing app and tests, (e) choose a minimal end-to-end workflow, (f) specify safety/offline constraints, (g) measure adoption and outcome. Preserve relevant existing workflows rather than deleting features on theoretical grounds.

## Output
One-page product decision memo: persona, current pain, value mechanism, experience steps, missing evidence, alternative/competition, minimum acceptance, dependencies, deliberate exclusions and next falsification test. No feature marked 'shipped' from a mockup.
