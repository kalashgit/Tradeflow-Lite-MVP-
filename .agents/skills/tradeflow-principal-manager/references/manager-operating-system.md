# Manager Operating System — TradeFlow
## Authority order
Founder-approved decisions and actual market observations define intent. Current source and exact-SHA CI define built behavior. Original roadmap and one ledger define sequencing. Historical handoffs are context, not proof.
## Decision memo
Record **ID | customer | problem | observed evidence | hypothesis | alternatives | recommendation | scope owner | prerequisites | required approvals | measure/stop rule**. If two sources conflict, show both and seek resolution; do not quietly invent a consensus.
## Default prioritization
Safety and data integrity > preserved existing customer workflows > critical end-to-end job costing/invoice-readiness > pilot usability and real customer evidence > voice sophistication or speculative broad platform additions. Priorities are adjustable after real evidence, not personal taste.
## Manager's value filter
- Who pays, who uses, what decision becomes better?
- Does the workflow move verified labor/material/economic evidence to an owner action, with least possible data entry?
- Why is incumbent software inadequate for that specific buyer?
- Which cheaper alternative or manual process could solve it?
- Will the feature increase switching cost, onboarding burden, model cost or support labor?
- What would disprove the value claim?
## Delegation cadence
One principal engineering writer on current branch, bounded tasks, independent test/QA review and read-only research. Do not launch imaginary workers. Before work, reconcile status; afterward record exact commit and gates. Engineering status and commercial validation are separate.
## Founder approval queue
Price/contract promise, customer outreach, identity/role change, public preview, production deployment, PR merge, live finance, paid external compute, data-sharing and model licensing decisions require explicit review by responsible owner.
## Five-task daily report
Product delivery / AI capability / verification / customer evidence / sales-and-commercial; each has owner, proof, time budget or size, blocker and outcome. Report 'no progress' if genuinely none.
## Success definition
The owner can use a trustworthy simple business headquarters and pay for it because it produces independently measurable advantage with reasonable onboarding and support cost. Do not equate demo polish or test count with this outcome.
