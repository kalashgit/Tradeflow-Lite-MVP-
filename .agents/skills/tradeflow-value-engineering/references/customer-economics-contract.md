# Value Proof and Economics Contract
## Contractor outcome classes
1. Job cost reliability (hours/material spend actually attributed and verified).
2. Change-order recoverability (unapproved/unsent/unbilled status, not booked income).
3. Invoice readiness and days-to-invoice (job complete does not imply billable without signatures).
4. Collections evidence (issued invoices, recorded receipts, missing bank settlement).
5. Owner admin time and work quality (callbacks, errors, field friction).
## Metric examples
- Labor-hour variance = recorded actual hours − budget hours. Do not treat incomplete time capture as full actual.
- Direct-cost margin = (eligible revenue − validated direct costs) / eligible revenue, only when revenue/cost and scope comparable; distinguish margin vs markup, tax, committed vs spent vs invoiced.
- Unbilled-ready value must be computed from authorized, approved scope and invoicing eligibility; an unapproved change order is not receivable.
- Days-to-invoice = timestamp invoice issued − timestamp job actually eligible for billing, only with reliable timestamps.
- Buyer ROI must use measured time or recovered cash effects, not unsupported claim that workflow 'made $X'.
## Internal economics
Track monthly realized subscription income, activation and retention, onboarding/support cost, LLM/voice cost, other hosting/integration costs and sales time. Compare contribution after variable costs with actual acquisition cost; avoid claiming 'CAC' from hypothetical conversion rates.
## Experiment sheet
Hypothesis; baseline population and dates; data source/missing; intervention and alternatives; leading signal; downside/quality counter-metric; expected range explicitly illustrative; actual delta; confounders; willingness-to-pay evidence; go/stop rule.
## Value-ranked engineering
When two features compete, prefer smaller first-value time, lower human data-entry burden, truthful source lineage and faster controlled measurement; reject a flashy feature with no buyer job.
## Finance authority
No free-form generated amounts, settlement claims or payments. Deterministic finance belongs to existing engine + tradeflow-financial-engineer. Always make unknowns visible.
